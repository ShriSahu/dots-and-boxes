Privacy Policy — Dots & Boxes

Last updated: July 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Dots & Boxes mobile app ("the App", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and your choices. Dots & Boxes is a casual pencil-and-paper style puzzle game with an optional online multiplayer mode.

1. Information We Collect

The App uses Firebase (a Google service) for its optional online multiplayer features, coin rewards, and cross-device stat sync. We collect only the following:

DataPurposePersonally identifiable?
Anonymous Firebase Authentication UID Identifies your device/session so online games, coin balances, and stats can be associated with you across app launches. No — generated automatically, not linked to your name, email, or any account.
Game statistics Wins, losses, draws, and games played — used to show your stats and (optionally) a leaderboard. No
Coin balance & transaction history In-game virtual currency earned by playing (no real-money purchases) — used to unlock cosmetic board themes. No
Online room/game data Moves, scores, and player display name you optionally set — used only to sync a live match between you and your opponent in real time. No — display names are user-chosen nicknames, not verified identities.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or any government-issued identifiers. We do not require account creation, sign-in with email/Google/Apple, or any personal profile.

2. How Information Is Used

  • To let two players connect and play a real-time online match.
  • To save your local preferences (player name, grid size, timer setting) and game statistics so they persist between sessions.
  • To track and award in-game coins for wins, draws, and daily play, and to record which cosmetic themes you've unlocked.
  • To show optional leaderboards of anonymous player stats.

3. Data Storage

Data is stored in Google Firebase / Firestore and locally on your device via standard app storage (AsyncStorage). Firebase security rules restrict each user to reading and writing only their own records; see our public firestore.rules for the exact rules enforced server-side.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or data brokers. The only third party involved is Google Firebase, which acts purely as our backend infrastructure provider (authentication + database hosting). Firebase's own privacy practices are described at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

5. Advertising

The App contains no advertising and no third-party ad SDKs.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because all identifiers are anonymous and no personal data is requested, the App is suitable for a general audience.

7. Your Choices & Data Deletion

  • You can play entirely offline (local 2-player or vs. AI) without any data leaving your device.
  • Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored preferences and stats from your device.
  • To request deletion of your anonymous Firebase data (UID, stats, coin balance), contact us using the email below with your in-app display name and approximate last-played date.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date above.
